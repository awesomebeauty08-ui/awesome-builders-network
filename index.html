INTRODUCTION

A network built to connect the people and businesses that build our world.

Awesome Builders Network (ABN-7742) is a structured industry network created to connect businesses and individuals across the building, construction, engineering, infrastructure and related sectors.

ABN provides a platform where businesses can build visibility, present their services and products, and develop useful industry connections. Professionals can establish their professional presence, showcase relevant expertise and connect with other industry participants.

Technical practitioners and skilled tradespeople are also recognised as important contributors to the industry. ABN provides distinct membership pathways that reflect the applicant's actual business, professional or trade identity.

PROFESSIONAL RECOGNITION

Expertise deserves accurate representation.

ABN recognises professional identity while respecting the different registration, licensing and certification requirements that apply across occupations and jurisdictions.

Registered or Licensed

For individuals whose relevant registration or licence has been checked with the appropriate professional or regulatory body, where applicable.

Certified or Qualified

For individuals presenting relevant academic, technical, vocational or professional certificates that have been reviewed.

Experienced Practitioner

For experienced practitioners whose work history and relevant evidence have been reviewed. Experience alone does not imply statutory registration or licensing.

ABN membership does not itself grant a professional licence, statutory registration or permission to practise a regulated occupation. Credential claims are subject to verification.
